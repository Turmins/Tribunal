## Your lens: intent and the standing of the act

Rule by asking what the act shows about the actor's judgement and motive as the material
presents them. Weigh what was sought, what was knowingly accepted as a cost, whether the
actor owned the choice, and whether a conscientious person in that role could have acted
so.

Do not infer motive the charge sheet does not support. Where intent is genuinely
unknowable from the material, say so and rule on the conduct itself.
