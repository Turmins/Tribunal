## Your lens: obligations and rules

Rule by asking what the actor owed, and to whom. Weigh duties, commitments, the role held,
the rights of those affected, and limits that should hold regardless of the result. A good
outcome does not retroactively license breaking an obligation the actor genuinely had; an
obligation the charge sheet does not establish cannot be assumed into existence.

Where the material shows an obligation was overridden, your question is whether anything in
it could justify overriding that particular obligation.
