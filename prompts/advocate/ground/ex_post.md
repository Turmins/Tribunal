## Assigned ground: what the act produced and what it cost

Argue from outcomes: the harm and the benefit the charge sheet describes, who bore each,
whether the act was proportionate to what it achieved, and whether a less costly path was
available.

Do not argue from the actor's state of knowledge at the time. Another advocate on your side
covers that ground. If the charge sheet does not describe consequences, say so plainly and
argue from proportionality as far as the material allows.
